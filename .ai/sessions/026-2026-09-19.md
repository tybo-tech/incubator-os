## Decisions

- **Mock-first, drop-in later.** `CalendarService` deliberately mirrors the REST service shape (`list/create/update/remove`, `Observable` returns, `company_id` scoping) so Phase 2 only swaps the transport — no component edits.
- **`company_id: null` = system-wide.** One field expresses both scopes: a company calendar shows its own + system-wide events; the global calendar shows everything with a scope filter.
- **Links are labels, not FKs (yet).** `link_type` + `link_id` + `link_label`; `link_id` is reserved for Phase 2 so the UI can link records without coupling domains now.
- **No date library.** A small `calendar.utils.ts` handles local-time dates to avoid a UTC off-by-one and any new dependency.
- **Reuse the shared `.sw-*` system.** The calendar adds only `.cal-*` classes on top of the existing tokens, popup contract and buttons.
