## Decisions

- **Transactional single request** for create/link (target + binding + provenance), per the spec, instead of two client calls.
- **Measure options limited to approved definitions** (Export, Combined); the domestic-only measure is deferred pending definition approval — no invented measure.
- **Preview reuses the measurement service** so the prefill is the same calculation used at verification; warnings mirror the `no_accounts` / `partial_coverage` / `unknown` / `incomplete` states.
- **Periods validated server-side** before insert (invalid period → 400, nothing created).
- **Single manual provenance row per target** (link is idempotent; no duplicates).
- **Component is standalone/OnPush/signals** and embedded in the (legacy) revenue screen rather than converting that screen to OnPush, avoiding destabilisation.
