## Decisions

- **Three explicit scopes** rather than one mixed list: Results & achievements / Decisions / Awaiting review — matching the required separation, with decisions labelled as events and excluded from totals.
- **Capability gating mirrors backend state**: drafts (unverified) → edit/delete + evidence; verified/decided → immutable in the UI; SA → verify/reject/revoke/supersede; other company users → draft authoring only.
- **Decisions render an "Event" state** (never a verification state) since they cannot be verified.
- **Evidence add is UI-gated to drafts** even though the backend permits append on verified records — the UI presents verified records as immutable.
- **Measurable numbers come from the authoritative snapshot** at verification; manual numeric entry is a fallback only when no measure is configured.
- **Grouped view groups by category** (consistent across scopes).
- Reused the existing normalized workspace conventions wholesale (shared stylesheet, `app-icon`, table/grouped/popup pattern) rather than inventing new UI.
