## Decisions

- **Profile is the source of truth.** Sector, description, buyer type, target market, products and
  pricing become profile facts; forms/submissions may propose but never silently overwrite.
- **Sector reuses `industries`.** No second taxonomy; `companies.industry_id` is surfaced and scored.
- **Products get their own table** (`company_products_services`) with price, basis, active flag and
  order. Deactivate, never hard delete.
- **Completeness is derived** (100-point weighted contract with stable gap codes) and shared by the
  profile, Overview and dashboard — one PHP implementation.
- **Submissions are reviewed, not auto-applied** (D3): token link → draft → submit → diff →
  apply/reject, with conflicts blocking a blind apply.
- **"Coach" is not a new auth role** (D1): `company_assignments` carries the user↔company relationship;
  roles stay System Administrator / Coordinator / Judge / Advisor / Director / Staff.
- **Health is explainable** (D4): five weighted components, each with reasons and evidence; snapshots
  are a forward-only trend log, not the score.
- **Reports are server-generated** (D5) through the existing HTML→PDF service, stored privately,
  served by signed token, with an HTML fallback.
- **Sprint order is gated**: 013 → 014 → 015 → 016; 015 must not expose health before assignments and
  the access matrix exist.
