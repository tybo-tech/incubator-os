## Decisions

- **Phase 1 is schema + vocabulary only.** `feature.json` is deliberately **not** updated yet: declaring
  visit queries/commands before their endpoints exist would misrepresent the capability surface. They are
  added in Phase 2.
- **The enum widen is the only change to an existing table**, and it is additive (no reorder, no removal).
- **`report_version_no` on the sign-off** is the mechanism that stops an acknowledgement applying to a later
  re-issue.
- **Funding is not stored** on the report (derived on read in Phase 3).
- **U8 lives in Sprint 011 task 3.8**, before the first new Session-link write, not only in Sprint 012.
