## Decisions

- **A dedicated append-only `session_activities` table**, because `recent_activities`
  is a flat, unindexed, mutable dashboard feed with an `INT` `reference_id` and no
  structured payload — it cannot represent a Session timeline. The dashboard feed
  is still written (`module='sessions'`).
- **Dependency inversion for `SESSION_LINKED`**: Calendar declares
  `CalendarSessionGuard`; Sessions implements it. Calendar never imports Sessions.
- **Session stores no schedule**: rescheduling is the calendar's job, enforced by
  the absence of date/time columns on `sessions`.
- **Reopening deferred**: no auditable reopen mechanism exists in the repo, so it
  was not invented. Frozen means frozen; the activity table is the future hook.
- **Completed-Session reopen and Google/Outlook sync, recurrence, reminders,
  transcription, AI summaries and email notifications are explicitly out of scope.**
- `create` and `convert` are atomic with the calendar event; the Session gateway
  deliberately does not open its own transaction so the caller owns rollback.
